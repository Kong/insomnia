# Kind Communication Guidelines

This project follows the spirit of the [GNU Kind Communication Guidelines](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/kind-communication.html).
They apply to issues, pull requests, code review and any other project space.

- Assume good faith. Most mistakes are honest ones.
- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Criticize ideas without insulting anyone.
- Stay on topic and accept that a decision may not go your way.
- Respect that people contribute in their own time and have other things going on.
- Do not harass, threaten or demean anyone, or post their private information.

If someone's conduct is a problem, first ask them to stop if you feel able to. Otherwise,
[contact the maintainer](https://github.com/outmanos). The maintainer may edit or remove comments and block people who
keep breaking these guidelines.
